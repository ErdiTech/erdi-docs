## First reading in five minutes

1. Pick a model: [choose a module](get-started/choose-a-module.mdx). Check supply voltage first, it differs a lot between models.
2. Wire it: [wiring and power](get-started/wiring-and-power.md). Cross TX and RX, share ground.
3. Read a distance: [first reading with a USB-TTL adapter](get-started/first-reading-usb-ttl.md), or open the [serial monitor](tools/serial-monitor.mdx) in Chrome or Edge.

## Compatibility at a glance

We only write Verified where there is evidence you can open. Everything else says Untested or No driver yet. Details and the meaning of each status are on the [compatibility page](compatibility/index.mdx).

## Models without a public protocol

Some models are not documented here yet. Their protocol is provided on request: see [other models](models/more-models.md).

## Where the content comes from

Specifications and protocol fields are generated from ERDI's verified product database, and the protocol and guide pages are the documents in the open-source [erdi-lrf-drivers](https://github.com/ErdiTech/erdi-lrf-drivers) repository. Prices are not shown here, the buy button on each model page goes to the shop.
